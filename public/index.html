🧩 Chrome & Edge Application Autofill

Easily autofill any job portal (Workday, Personio, Lever, Taleo, Greenhouse, etc.) in 1 click.

FASTEST • ZERO INSTALLATION

Method 1: Instant Drag & Drop Bookmarklet

Drag this button directly to your Chrome or Edge Bookmarks Bar:

⚡ AeroApply Autofill ← Drag this button to your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B to show bar)

Whenever you open any job application portal, just click ⚡ AeroApply Autofill in your bookmarks bar and the entire form fills in automatically with your details!

BROWSER EXTENSION (EDGE & CHROME)

Method 2: Unpacked Browser Extension

The project includes a ready-to-load Manifest V3 extension inside the extension folder:

  1. In Chrome or Edge, navigate to chrome://extensions or edge://extensions
  2. Enable Developer mode (toggle in top-right or left sidebar).
  3. Click Load unpacked button.
  4. Select the folder: C:\Job Applying Agent\extension

Once loaded, a floating "⚡ Autofill with AeroApply" button appears on every job portal!
